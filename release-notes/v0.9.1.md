# Where to Buy 0.9.1

More store logos.

## What's new

- **The store logos download now covers 13 stores.** Etsy, Barnes & Noble, The Home Depot, Lowe's, Costco, Petco and PetSmart join Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, Target, Chewy and eBay. Petco and Barnes & Noble also come in white for dark buttons.
- **Add logos from your computer** matches the new logos to those stores too.

REI and Bookshop.org aren't included, because there are no freely licensed copies of their logos to share. Use your own image for them, or leave them as text buttons.

Get the logos from `where-to-buy-store-logos.zip` below. As before, they're public domain files from Wikimedia Commons but still the stores' trademarks; the README in the zip lists where each came from.

## Install

Download `where-to-buy-0.9.1.zip` below, then in WordPress go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin** and choose **Replace current with uploaded**. Your items and settings are kept. See the [guide](https://bignall.github.io/where-to-buy/guide.html).

Requires PHP 8.1+ and WordPress 6.4+.
