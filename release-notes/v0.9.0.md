# Where to Buy 0.9.0

Adding stores and their logos is quicker.

## What's new

- **Pick a store from a list.** **Add store** now shows the stores we know the details for: Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, Target, Chewy, eBay, Etsy, Bookshop.org, Barnes & Noble, The Home Depot, Lowe's, Costco, Petco, PetSmart and REI. Pick one and its domains are filled in (Amazon comes with its tag setting and Associates disclosure too). Stores you already have are crossed out. **Blank store** adds one of your own.
- **Upload logos from your computer.** Each store's button image now has an **Upload** button: pick a file and it's added to your Media Library and used, with no trip to the Media Library first. **Choose** still picks one that's already there.
- **Add all the logos at once.** Download the [store logos](https://github.com/bignall/where-to-buy/releases/latest/download/where-to-buy-store-logos.zip), unzip it, then click **Add logos from your computer** in Store Settings and select the logos. Each one goes to the store it's named after, using each store's main logo when you select several versions. Click **Save Changes** to keep them.

## Install

Download `where-to-buy-0.9.0.zip` below, then in WordPress go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin** and choose **Replace current with uploaded**. Your items and settings are kept. See the [guide](https://bignall.github.io/where-to-buy/guide.html).

Requires PHP 8.1+ and WordPress 6.4+.
